### Overview

This project focuses on **end-to-end simulation scene generation driven by natural language**, enabling users to create complex 3D environments directly from textual descriptions.

### Role

- **Technical Lead**

### Funding Source

- **Guangdong Flagship Project**

### Contributions

- **Architected the full pipeline** for text-driven scene generation, from language understanding to 3D scene synthesis.
- **Integrated Unreal Engine (UE)** with **2D Gaussian Splatting**, automating the construction of high-fidelity 3D scenes from natural language descriptions.
- Designed reusable modules for **scene layout, asset placement, and lighting control**, making the system extensible to new environments and tasks.

### Techniques & Tools

- Natural Language Processing for scene and object specification
- Unreal Engine (UE) for high-fidelity simulation
- 2D Gaussian Splatting for fast, realistic rendering of complex scenes
